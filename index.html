Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our honest working process.

Are your reviews safe for my app/business?
We use 100% manual method - real search, real install, real review. It's the safest method in the market. But we cannot give you 100% guarantee because Google Play / Map policy changes anytime and it's not our property. We always follow the safest way possible.
Will the reviews ever drop or disappear?
Reviews can drop anytime due to Google's algorithm. That's why our system is different - we charge you ONLY for reviews that are currently live. If any review drops, you don't need to pay for it. So no tension about drop.
How long does it take to complete an order?
We deliver slowly to keep it natural and safe, usually 2-5 reviews per day. The exact timeline depends on your app's current condition. We will give you a clear idea before starting.
What payment methods do you accept?
We ONLY accept Bkash, Nagad, USDT (TRC20/BEP20), and Binance Pay / Binance Balance. We don't accept PayPal, Payoneer, Wise or Bank directly. But you can deposit to Binance from any crypto wallet and then pay us easily.
Do you provide proof/screenshots of work?
Yes. We provide a daily report with all published review details. You can check the report, count the live reviews, and pay only for those live ones. Full transparency.
Can you handle bulk orders?
Yes, we can handle bulk orders. But we still deliver them slowly and manually to keep it safe. Contact us for your bulk requirement and we will make a daily plan for you.
